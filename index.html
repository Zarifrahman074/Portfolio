About

Turning field research into financial products

I'm a financial inclusion and digital financial services professional with over two years of experience in Bangladesh's agri-fintech sector. I lead OGRO, an end-to-end platform that tracks the farmer journey across financing, quality inputs, market access, and agricultural advisory — reaching 15,000+ farmers to date. I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate of KUET with professional English proficiency (IELTS 7.0), and I'm looking to bring that same blend of field-tested empathy and rigorous analysis to financial, social, and economic inclusion work.
